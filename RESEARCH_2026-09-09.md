# Dreative: latest-build audit and production research

## Verdict

The latest completed build fails the requested immersive, motion-led direction.
It has better product material, but the experience still concentrates its design
effort in one pixel/zoom scene. That scene does not develop into an equally
considered shopping interaction, informational sequence, or ending. The previous
skill revision did not solve the complete-route problem in this run.

The independent verdict for `clothing-motion__with-a__202609081112`, built with
`git:401798c`, is overall **3/5**, material and subject **4/5**, and motion, craft,
and structure **2/5**. It is a completed build, not the earlier prototype verdict.
[User verdict](C:/Users/lty/Downloads/dreative-testbed/archive/202609081112/clothing-motion/verdict.json).

The provider change is a variable, not an excuse. This evidence cannot isolate
its causal contribution. Dreative should make its method usable across providers;
it cannot guarantee identical judgment or tool capabilities across them.

## Findings, in order of consequence

| Finding | Evidence and consequence | Design/engineering response |
|---|---|---|
| The experience is organized around an effect, with ordinary sections afterward | The pixelated product scene releases into repeated product cards, a size table, care/shipping rows, reviews, and footer. The source grouping has effectively become the page architecture. | Compose the visitor's journey with actual content before choosing the focal effect. Let useful comparison, evidence, and choice determine the middle. |
| The opening postpones the subject | The desktop masthead occupies roughly a viewport with gradient and type before the product scene. In the sampled composition it supplies little material evidence or interaction to justify that space. | Decide what the visitor encounters immediately: the object, its world, a useful preview, or a meaningful action. Do not fill time with a fake loader. |
| Framing is under-authored | The canvas samples the photo differently from the sharp DOM image, which also has its own object-position and scale. The changing frame crops the model awkwardly in intermediate states. | Author the frame and inner image together; share geometry across renderers and inspect landmarks through the join. Recompose on mobile. |
| Motion changes appearance more than understanding | Pixelation and enlargement work on one photograph. They cannot reveal unseen construction, another view, or physical behavior. | Acquire the material required by the story: detail photography, actual footage, a coherent render, or an explanatory graphic when justified. Video is an option, not a required badge. |
| The shopping layout has little development | Repeated large images, specifications, and controls dominate the middle. Card entrances run on mount and can finish before the visitor reaches them. | Compare a direct collection with contextual detail, an image-led index, or another useful exploration model. Trigger entrances when encountered if they serve that choice. |
| Primary-task polish is incomplete | Checkout has no handler. The bag is an aside with aria-hidden rather than complete modal focus behavior; closed descendants are not made inert. Clipped scene controls also need inactive-state handling. | Test the final action and state lifecycle, including Escape, focus return, rapid changes, touch, and keyboard. Explicitly represent a prototype endpoint when no transaction exists. |
| The ending is an isolated typographic composition | Large footer type has visual potential, but does not resolve the earlier scene or establish a memorable final relationship. | Author its relationship to the opening and useful next action. It may be quiet; it still needs composition. |

These are contextual judgments, not universal bans on gradients, cards, tables,
stills, or pixelation. A table can be the best comparison tool. The failure is
that the complete experience does not meet this brief's ambition.

## What the production research changes

The most useful references expose decisions and implementation together.
Podium's creators explain a selected media item becoming the next route's hero;
they also rejected an effect that competed with the footage. This gives Dreative
a concrete content-to-navigation relationship to learn from. I observed changing
opening compositions on the original site; the route implementation account is
the creators' evidence, not an assertion of my own complete route testing.
[Podium production account](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/06/23/podium-building-a-website-where-running-becomes-storytelling/).

ZERO's production account links its changing metaphor to a transition from
watching to exploration and discusses the asset/rendering work required to
support it. The transferable lesson is to develop meaning and agency together.
It does not make destruction or an elaborate 3D world a default.
[ZERO production account](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/07/17/zero-the-engineering-behind-a-defiant-interactive-narrative/).

For local interaction quality, paired comparisons are more educational than a
list of adjectives. Emil Kowalski's exercises compare concrete motion properties;
his source skills provide focused implementation/review routes. Dreative can use
these to diagnose a specific control without importing a foreign workflow.
[Exercises](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/train-your-judgement),
[source skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills).

MengTo's scroll-sequence skill separates normalized scroll progress from the
renderer and calls out focal points, direct reversibility, loading, and cleanup.
That architecture is useful across DOM, video, canvas, and spatial material.
Its demo is a worked example, not an appropriate page anatomy for every project.
[Original skill](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/web-design/scroll-scrubbed-visual-sequence/SKILL.md).

Impeccable contributes explicit design diagnosis and vocabulary. The local taste
skill also starts from audience and brief, but its numerical defaults and blanket
stylistic prescriptions should not become Dreative's universal aesthetic.
[Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/),
[local taste skill](C:/Users/lty/.codex/skills/design-taste-frontend/SKILL.md).

## Resource decisions

The supplied names span discovery, code, media, authoring, and advice. Those
outputs are different. A preview is not a licensed download; a component is not
a complete experience; a service homepage is not a callable agent tool.

| Resource | Useful output and adoption decision |
|---|---|
| Recent Design / Godly | Scout composition and motion, then visit the original. Recent Design was accessible, but several embedded videos would not play during this study. Do not claim their timing was inspected. [Recent Design](https://recent.design/) |
| Skiper UI | Adapt an actual selected component. Its guide requires attribution for free use, unlike Pro. Inspect dependencies rather than installing its whole ecosystem. [Official guide](https://skiper-ui.com/docs/quick-start) |
| Aceternity UI | Component implementations can answer a concrete interaction need. Choose a primitive, replace demo content, and own its integration. [Official catalogue](https://ui.aceternity.com/components) |
| Originkit | Tunable source delivery, with account/allowance constraints. Previewing is separate from obtaining code; editor MCP needs credentials. The canonical source is originkit.dev, distinct from Origin UI. [Official component workflow](https://www.originkit.dev/docs/components) |
| Spline | Scene authoring/export is useful when an actual scene supports the concept. Code Tab behavior is not exported through Three.js/R3F targets; validate export compatibility early. [Export limitation](https://docs.spline.design/interaction-states-events-and-actions/code-tab), [optimization](https://docs.spline.design/exporting-your-scene/how-to-optimize-your-scene) |
| React Three Fiber | A renderer for authored spatial material, not a model or art director. Demand rendering needs invalidation when imperative changes occur; adapt performance with actual observed behavior. [Official performance guidance](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/advanced/scaling-performance) |
| ShaderGradient | An authored material with configurable camera/color behavior. Core rendering and editor UI are separate; check peer compatibility. [Original repository](https://github.com/ruucm/shadergradient) |
| Paper / liquid logo | Useful image-mask material treatment. Paper Shaders uses Apache-2.0; liquid-logo uses PolyForm Shield. Treat their terms separately and use an authorized logo. [Shaders](https://github.com/paper-design/shaders), [liquid-logo license](https://github.com/paper-design/liquid-logo/blob/main/LICENSE) |
| liquid-glass-js | Experimental WebGL/background-capture treatment. Its roadmap still includes mobile, accessibility, and performance work. Prove the actual background and device use before adoption. [Original repository](https://github.com/dashersw/liquid-glass-js) |
| particles.casberry.in | Authoring/export candidate with visible import/export affordances. No export was integrated or license established in this audit. [Original tool](https://particles.casberry.in/) |
| Poly Haven | A concrete source of models, textures, and lighting; asset CC0 terms are distinct from website/API service terms. [Asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license) |
| Manus | Potential asset/design production service. No callable Manus tool or authorized paid workflow was established here. Do not prescribe it as a dependency. [Official design tools](https://manus.im/tools/ai-design) |
| ion-design repositories | The name is not one library: design-crit collects narrated human review with source context; ditto.site describes capture-to-code. Neither is proof of original art direction or permission to reuse a site's media. [design-crit](https://github.com/ion-design/design-crit), [ditto.site](https://github.com/ion-design/ditto.site) |
| SPRRRINT | Free Figma components and gradient discovery can aid composition; verify the selected download and terms. They are not automatically React components. [Free collection](https://sprrrint.com/free) |

Kokonut, Bklit, Uiverse, Lukacho, Watermelon, UILORA, and AnimMaster remain
targeted discovery routes in the existing resource guide. This audit did not
integrate every component or establish blanket licensing for them. Motion,
Anime.js, and GSAP remain orchestration choices, selected for the mechanism
rather than counted as signs of sophistication.

Refero and Appshots returned limited extractable content; Grayblocks and Mockuply
were discovery leads for kits/mockups, not verified runtime assets. The 10x.app
page identified an app builder, not a footage library. uiprompts.app could not
be inspected reliably. A websiteprompts repository candidate failed primary
retrieval. BLANK, VARIANT, STYLES, and the SKIPPPER spelling were not uniquely
resolved. These gaps are recorded rather than filled with guessed install commands.

## Implemented changes

1. Moved whole-experience composition before focal-effect selection in SKILL.md
   and PLAN.md. The decisive prototype now tests a join within that intended
   journey, instead of defining the journey after the hero succeeds.
2. Added conditional production studies with original sources and explicit
   transfer decisions. Added a construction guide for image ownership, crop,
   canvas/DOM continuity, interruption, resizing, and destination usability.
3. Added `mediaPlacement` and optional static/responsive framing to the existing
   sequence renderer. Centered cover remains compatible by default. The helper
   supports cover/contain with normalized alignment; it does not select an
   aesthetic focal point or implement the entire CSS object-position grammar.
4. Added diagnosis-specific corrections for empty openings, unavailable detail,
   crop jumps, offscreen mount entrances, unrelated endings, and hidden controls.
   Updated resource routing with actual outputs, access limits, and reuse distinctions.
5. Recorded the latest independent failure against the existing lesson. The
   revised approach remains proposed until a later real run earns an external verdict.

The prior revision's media-inspection CLI remains useful for footage contact
sheets and shot inspection. No additional effect catalogue, compulsory video,
paid service, style quota, or automatic taste score was added. The historical
testbed build and verdict remain unchanged.

## Verification and remaining uncertainty

The renderer is checked against actual CSS image output through responsive
resize, as well as aspect preservation, cover/contain bounds, normalized
alignment, and invalid geometry. Existing tests cover frame races, cache behavior,
missing media, disposal, and other native foundations.

Executed successfully on the revised source:

- `npm run test:active`: production CLI build, 78 unit tests, 8 native-foundation
  browser tests, and 29 capture/visual-smoke browser tests passed.
- `dreative docs-check`: packaged references and metadata passed.
- Skill Creator's `quick_validate.py`: valid skill.
- `git diff --check`: clean patch formatting.
- Exact local Codex installation and `install-skill --codex --check`: passed.
  Both new references are installed; the maintainer-only dogfood record is excluded.

This is skill/runtime maintenance, not a new frontend delivery. No frontend
finalization marker or self-awarded taste verdict is claimed for the archived site.

The testbed's `git:HEAD` option extracts committed files from the sibling Dreative
repository. Once this revision is committed, that default selects the new skill.
For reproducible comparisons, select its exact commit. A direct directory source
(`C:\Users\lty\Downloads\Dreative\skill\dreative`) instead tests the working tree.
The locally installed skill and the testbed's explicit Git source are separate
choices; keep the testbed's Codex and Claude installations synchronized too.

These checks cannot show that an autonomous provider will now produce an
excellent complete website. The decisive follow-up remains a fresh, held-out
build using the revised skill and an independent full-route playback verdict.
Compare old/new revisions with matched provider, tools, budget, and task when
estimating improvement; retain failures and incomplete runs. Do not add another
rule merely because a self-review says the new prose sounds stronger.
