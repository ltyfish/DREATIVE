# Learn from complete productions

Use a relevant study when the idea or handoff is still vague. Open the original
and inspect the passage you intend to learn from. The notes distinguish an
author's production account from behavior you have personally observed. These
are examples of reasoning, not page templates or assets to copy.

## Podium: selected media becomes the destination

The creators describe duplicating selected project media into an aligned overlay,
expanding it while the route changes, then resolving it into the next hero. Their
grain and typographic behavior support a consistent world. They also describe
rejecting a heavier pixel transition because it competed with the footage.
[Production account](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/06/23/podium-building-a-website-where-running-becomes-storytelling/)
and [original site](https://podium.global/).

**Transfer:** identify what the visitor selected and make its destination real.
Build that destination before tuning the join. Inspect matching image crops,
reverse navigation, and the instant visible ownership changes; `MEDIA_HANDOFF.md`
explains the geometry. The useful lesson is a relationship between content and
navigation, not a requirement for grain, pixelation, or a sports aesthetic.

## ZERO: the metaphor changes the visitor's role

The creators describe a degree-promise narrative moving through shattered material,
evidence on fragments, shredded certificates, a tunnel, and an explorable city.
They discuss segmented progress, scene lifecycles, asset preparation, texture
uploads, and shader warmup as part of making that sequence feasible.
[Production account](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/07/17/zero-the-engineering-behind-a-defiant-interactive-narrative/)
and [original site](https://why.zero.university/).

**Transfer:** choose a metaphor the subject can sustain, then change what the
visitor understands or controls as it develops. Prototype the actual expensive
assets and transition into exploration early. Their reported performance is an
author account, not a benchmark for your device. Destruction, tunnels, and 3D
cities are not universal storytelling requirements.

## NITEX: identity across story and action

The studio describes a fashion/technology identity expressed through angular
framing, energetic photography and generated visuals. Narrative chapters coexist
with direct practical paths; addressable overlays expose detail without requiring
every visitor to finish the story. The reported stack combines GSAP with native
transitions and Web Animations.
[Production account](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/10/10/nitex-building-a-brand-and-digital-platform-for-fashions-new-supply-chain/)
and [original site](https://nitex.com/).

**Transfer:** build a recognizable motion and material language across narrative,
navigation and working states while leaving the primary task directly accessible.
The account is evidence of the creators' method, not a verified current playback
or an instruction to copy its angular shapes. Operate the live passage at use.

## Interaction craft: compare the property that matters

[Emil Kowalski's paired exercises](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/train-your-judgement)
let the reader compare element size, easing, entrances, popovers, stagger, and
layered motion. Use a relevant comparison to sharpen a specific judgment in
playback. His [source skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills) offer focused
implementation and review guidance. Do not import a whole external workflow
or mistake a rule suitable for a frequent control for the pacing of a filmic scene.

## Turn the study into a build decision

Describe the material, what changes on input, what stays identifiable, the useful
resolved state, and the technical ownership. Then adapt with your own content:

- If evidence is missing, source or author it before building the transition.
- If the existing material supports only a still, compose an honest still-led
  experience or obtain a different medium. Do not promise a spatial reveal from
  a photograph that has no unseen geometry.
- If the prototype is visually weak, compare matched states with the reference.
  Diagnose scale, crop, information, staging, pace, or material separately; a
  different animation package does not repair an absent visual relationship.

Carry the successful mechanism into the actual route and judge the least-resolved
passage. Keep licenses for adopted code and assets separate from reference notes.
No minimum reference count, effect count, or library stack establishes quality.
