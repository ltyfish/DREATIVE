03 / Process as an interaction

Two plates.
One impression.

Motion reveals a relationship: two offset color plates register into one composition. This is an explanatory vector diagram, not a photorealistic press simulation.

Inspect the process

First, the red plate.

Isolate one ink to see its shape. The faint second plate supplies orientation.

Try changing your selection mid-transition. The plates move from their current position, with no queued entrance to wait through.

An idea needs a destination

Keep the relationship.
Change the subject.

Plan the material and resolved composition before generating. Use generated views as design studies, then build and inspect the actual behavior. No still image proves that a transition works.
