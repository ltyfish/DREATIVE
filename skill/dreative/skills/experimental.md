# Experimental

Use for a deliberate moment that changes how the reader perceives or handles the
product — where the interesting claim is not what the page says but how it
behaves.

## What makes it work rather than merely strange

**It has an expressive intention.** Start from the subject, the brand's attitude,
or a compelling technique that suggests a new way to experience them. Try the
relationship with real content. Delight, tension, play and surprise can justify
an experiment without a literal product metaphor.

**The reader can find the edges.** They should be able to work out what they are
doing, undo it, and get to the ordinary product from inside it. Legibility is
not a compromise imposed on the experiment — an experiment nobody can operate
produced no experience to evaluate.

**It has enough orientation to be usable.** Familiar controls can support an
unconventional composition. Several experiments can form a coherent world when
their material, input and rhythm relate; the whole route need not become a
conventional container around one unusual widget. Inspect whether they compete.

## Prototyping

Build the riskiest part first and in isolation, before the page depends on it.
The point is to find out early whether the idea is any good, which is worth
knowing while you can still change your mind. Most experimental ideas do not
survive being built, and that is the process working.

Explore live demos and implementation sources through
`../references/CREATIVE_RESOURCES.md` when they can expand the idea or resolve a
construction problem. Adapt a promising technique with the actual material and
destination. The native foundations are available helpers, not the boundary of
what to try. Stop collecting references once an experiment can answer the question.

## How this fails

Input responses without a perceivable payoff. Distortion that spoils the intended
subject. Physics that makes a control hard to use. Hidden navigation that strands
the visitor. These are observable failures, not bans on cursors, shaders or physics.

And the failure the reject-list above tends to cause on its own: a page with no
experiment in it, which trips none of these and was chosen precisely because it
trips none of these. Explore the risk with a small real experiment and judge the
experience it creates, including its expressive value.
