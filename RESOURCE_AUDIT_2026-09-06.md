## Generation policy implemented

Generation is an equal production route and may be the first choice. Judge fit,
coherence, controllability, and total production cost. A good still can support
several authored treatments; continuous subject motion needs coherent footage or
a render, not independently generated stills. Resolve a decisive shot before
spending on a whole set or long clip. For fictional concepts create consistent
concept assets; for real products preserve verified identity. Tool availability
must be demonstrated, not inferred from a service landing page.
